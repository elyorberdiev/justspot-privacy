Summary — the short version

  • The App never connects to a broker or exchange. It asks for no API keys, no broker password, and no bank or card details. Every trade is typed in by you.
  • You start signed in anonymously. You may optionally sign in with Google or Apple, which gives us your email address and display name.

  • We use Firebase and PostHog to understand crashes and aggregate usage. We do not show ads and we do not sell your data.
  • Delete Account, in the profile screen, erases your journal from both your device and our cloud, and then deletes the account itself — see section 11.

1. Introduction

JustSpot — Trading Journal ("App", "we", "our", "us") is developed and operated by Fazogirapps. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App handles, why, who else is involved, and what choices you have.

By downloading, installing, or using the App you agree to the practices described here. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

Contact: fazogirapps@gmail.com

2. Accounts and Sign-In

On first launch the App signs you in anonymously through Firebase Authentication, which issues a random identifier. Nothing about you is collected at that point.

You may then sign in with Google or Apple so that your journal is backed up and can be restored. When you do, we receive from that provider:

  • your email address (Apple users may choose Apple's private relay address instead);
  • your display name, where the provider supplies it;
  • the account identifier the provider issues.

We never receive your Google or Apple password. We do not collect your phone number, postal address, date of birth, precise location, contacts, calendar, or health data.

3. What the App Deliberately Does Not Collect

JustSpot has no broker or exchange integration. It does not ask for, store, or transmit broker credentials, exchange API keys, wallet keys or seed phrases, bank account details, or payment card numbers, and it cannot place, modify, or close a trade. If any screen or message ever asks you for such credentials in the name of JustSpot, it is not us — do not enter them, and contact us.

4. Information You Provide

4.1 Trade entries

For each trade you record, the App stores what you enter: the instrument or ticker symbol, the asset type, entry price, exit price, quantity, date, the strategy you used, the emotion you felt, the mistake you identify, whether you screened it for Shariah compliance, and your free-text notes. Profit and loss is calculated from those figures on your device.

4.2 Screenshots and images

You may attach an image to a trade — typically a chart screenshot — from your photo library or by taking a photo. The App accesses only the specific image you select or capture. It does not browse, index, or upload your photo library.

4.3 Journal notes

Any reflection or note you write in the App is stored the same way as the rest of the entry.

6. Where Your Data Is Stored

  • On your device. Your journal lives in a local database and works offline. This is the primary copy, and it is where an entry is written first.
  • In our cloud. Once you are signed in, entries are synced to Google Firebase — Cloud Firestore for the text and figures, Cloud Storage for attached images — filed under your account identifier.

Access is enforced by server-side security rules: a request carrying your identifier can read and write only data filed under that same identifier. All traffic uses HTTPS/TLS.

7. Subscriptions and Payments

JustSpot offers an optional auto-renewing subscription that unlocks premium features.

We never see or handle your card details. Payment is processed entirely by Apple or Google. We use RevenueCat, Inc. to verify purchases and tell the App whether your subscription is active. RevenueCat receives your app user identifier, the purchase receipt issued by the store, and basic device and country information.

Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period, in your App Store or Google Play subscription settings.

9. Device Permissions

  • Camera — only to photograph a chart or note when you choose the camera. Nothing is recorded in the background.
  • Photo library — only to let you attach an existing image. The App reads the single image you select.
  • Notifications — only to deliver reminders and announcements. You can decline and keep using the App.

You can withdraw any of these permissions at any time in your device settings.

10. Data Retention

  • Journal entries — kept until you delete them or delete your account. Deleting the account removes them from our cloud immediately, images included.
  • Firebase Analytics and PostHog events — retained for a limited period under our project settings, then deleted automatically.
  • Crashlytics — crash reports are retained by Google for approximately 90 days.
  • RevenueCat — purchase records are kept while the subscription and its history are relevant to the stores.

11. Your Rights and Choices

  • Delete a single trade — open it and choose Delete. It is removed from your device and from the cloud copy.
  • Delete your account — profile screen → Delete Account. This erases, in one step: every trade entry and attached image in our cloud, your user record including the notification token, the copy stored on your device, and finally your sign-in account itself. It cannot be undone. For security, Firebase requires a recent sign-in before an account can be deleted; if you are asked to sign in again, do so and repeat the deletion.
  • Remove the local copy — uninstall the App.
  • Cancel your subscription — in your App Store or Google Play settings. Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription; you must cancel it in the store.
  • Ask us anything else — write to fazogirapps@gmail.com. If you only ever used the App anonymously, we may be unable to locate your data unless you can supply your account identifier.
