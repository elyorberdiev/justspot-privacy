1. Introduction

Welcome to the JustSpot - Trading Journal App ("App", "we", "our", or "us") developed and operated by Fazogirapps. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, share, and protect your personal information when you use our App on Android and iOS platforms.

By downloading, installing, or using our App, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

Contact: fazogirapps@gmail.com

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide Directly

  • Email address (for account registration and authentication)
  • Display name or username
  • JustSpot - Trading Journal entries: trade date, instrument/ticker, entry price, exit price, position size, profit & loss (P&L), trade direction (long/short), and any notes or tags you add
  • Journal notes and written reflections you create within the App

We do not collect broker credentials, API keys, or connect to any brokerage accounts. All trade data is entered manually by you.

2.2 Information Collected Automatically

  • Device information: device model, operating system version (Android/iOS), unique device identifiers
  • App usage data: features used, session duration, screens visited
  • Crash reports and error logs
  • App version and language settings

2.3 User-Generated Content

  • Text notes, journal entries, and trade annotations stored locally on your device
  • For Premium users: the above content is also synced to Firebase Cloud (Google LLC) to enable cross-device access and backup

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

  • To provide, maintain, and improve the App's core features
  • To authenticate your account and enable secure login
  • To sync your journal data across devices (Premium users via Firebase)
  • To process and manage your subscription through RevenueCat
  • To verify in-app purchase entitlements on Google Play and Apple App Store
  • To diagnose technical issues and fix bugs using crash analytics
  • To respond to your support requests sent to fazogirapps@gmail.com
  • To send important service-related notifications (e.g., subscription renewal, policy updates)

We do not use your trading data for advertising, profiling, or selling to third parties.

4. Premium Subscription & In-App Purchases

The App offers a Premium version with additional features, available via:

  • Monthly or annual auto-renewing subscription on Android (Google Play Billing)
  • Monthly or annual auto-renewing subscription on iOS (Apple In-App Purchase)

All payment transactions are processed by the respective platform stores (Google Play or Apple App Store). We do not collect, process, or store your payment card details or banking information directly.

4.1 RevenueCat

We use RevenueCat, Inc. as our subscription management platform. RevenueCat receives purchase receipts and subscription status from Google Play and Apple to verify your entitlements and manage your Premium access.

RevenueCat may collect:

  • A pseudonymous user ID linked to your purchases
  • Purchase receipt data and subscription status
  • App and device identifiers for receipt validation

RevenueCat does not receive your financial or payment card data. For details, see RevenueCat's Privacy Policy at: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

4.2 Subscription Management

Auto-renewal: Subscriptions automatically renew unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period.

To cancel your subscription:

  • Android: Open Google Play Store → Profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions
  • iOS: Open Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions

Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period. We do not provide refunds for partial subscription periods unless required by applicable law.

Our App integrates the following third-party services. Each operates under its own privacy policy:

  • Firebase (Google LLC):
    • Firebase Authentication — secure user sign-in
    • Firebase Firestore — cloud sync of journal data for Premium users
    • Firebase Analytics — anonymous usage statistics
    • Firebase Crashlytics — crash and error reporting
    • Google's Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • PostHog, Inc. — Anonymous usage statistics and product analytics. (Privacy Policy: https://posthog.com/privacy)
  • RevenueCat, Inc.: Subscription and entitlement management for both Android and iOS (Privacy Policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy)
  • Google Play Billing (Google LLC): Payment processing for Android subscriptions (Google Payments Privacy Notice: https://payments.google.com/files/privacy.html)
  • Apple In-App Purchase (Apple Inc.): Payment processing for iOS subscriptions (Apple Privacy Policy: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/)

6. Data Storage & Security

6.1 Where Data Is Stored

  • Free users: All trade journal data is stored locally on your device only. We cannot access it.
  • Premium users: Data is synced to Firebase Cloud (Google Cloud infrastructure), with servers primarily located in the United States.

6.2 Security Measures

We implement industry-standard security practices including:

  • All data transmitted between your device and our servers is encrypted using HTTPS/TLS
  • Firebase Security Rules restrict access so only you can read/write your own data
  • We do not store passwords in plain text
  • Access to backend systems is restricted to authorized personnel only

While we take reasonable precautions, no method of data transmission or storage is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security of your data.

6.3 Data Retention

  • Account data is retained for as long as your account is active
  • After account deletion, your data is removed from our servers within 30 days
  • Local data on your device is deleted when you uninstall the App
  • Anonymized analytics data may be retained longer in aggregated, non-identifiable form
